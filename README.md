## About

This is my personal portfolio, designed to feel more like an **interactive notebook than a traditional portfolio website**.

Instead of presenting my work through standard cards and grids, I wanted visitors to experience my projects, skills, stories, and journey as they move through the pages of a personal creative journal.

The portfolio combines **design, storytelling, motion, and interaction** to create an experience that feels personal and memorable.

---

## Why a Notebook?

Ideas rarely begin as polished products. They start as **rough notes, sketches, experiments, mistakes, and improvements**.

That became the inspiration behind this portfolio.

The notebook represents the process behind the work. Handwritten elements represent thoughts and ideas, while the interactive pages showcase the things I have built, learned, and experienced.

The goal was simple: **make the portfolio feel like a reflection of the person behind it.**
